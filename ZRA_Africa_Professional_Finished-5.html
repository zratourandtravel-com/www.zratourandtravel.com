Frequently asked questions

Does ZRA guarantee a job?

No. We provide job-search and application support. Employment depends on employer requirements, competition, eligibility and the recruitment process.

Does ZRA guarantee a visa or work permit?

No. Immigration decisions are made by the relevant authorities. We can provide guidance on the process and requirements related to the opportunity.

What information should I provide?

Start with your name, contact details, education, skills, work experience, preferred country and the type of job you want. We will advise you if more information is needed.
