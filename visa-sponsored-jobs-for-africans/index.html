Understanding visa-sponsored jobs

“Visa-sponsored job” can mean different things depending on the country and employer. Some employers may sponsor or support a work authorization process for eligible candidates; others do not.

What ZRA checks

Employer identity

Look for a traceable employer or recognised recruitment route.

Job requirements

Qualifications, skills, experience, location and other conditions should be clear.

Sponsorship information

We describe sponsorship only when it is stated or otherwise verified for the specific opportunity.

Immigration pathway

Work authorization depends on the destination country's rules and the applicant's circumstances.

Possible job areas

Depending on current vacancies: healthcare, skilled trades, hospitality, chefs and cooks, motor mechanics, IT/software, agriculture, logistics, manufacturing and other roles.

What we do not promise

ZRA does not guarantee an employer sponsor, job offer, work permit or visa. The employer decides whom to hire and immigration authorities decide whether the relevant permission is granted.

Safety reminder: Do not pay anyone for a guaranteed job or guaranteed visa. Verify the employer, vacancy and official application route before making payments.
