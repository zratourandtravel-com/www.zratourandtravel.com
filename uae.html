UAE Job Application Support

Guidance for applicants interested in suitable UAE construction and cleaning opportunities. We assess applicant profiles and available recruitment routes before supporting an application.

WhatsApp ZRABack to International Services

Service areas

Construction

Construction-related roles may have employer-specific physical, experience and eligibility requirements. Check the exact vacancy requirements before applying.

Cleaning

Cleaning and housekeeping roles may be available to eligible male and female applicants, depending on the employer and vacancy requirements.

Application preparation

We help organise application details and submit through an employer’s official recruitment channel when available and with the applicant’s authorisation.

Documents that may be requested

Requirements vary by employer and role. Depending on the specific vacancy, applicants may be asked for:

Valid passport
Recent photograph
Medical examination or certificate, when required
Police clearance or certificate of good conduct, when required

Do not obtain or pay for documents until you have checked the actual vacancy and the relevant official requirements.

Important
This page describes ZRA’s support service; it does not announce a confirmed vacancy. Availability and overseas eligibility must be confirmed for each role. Employers make hiring decisions; immigration authorities decide visas and work permits. ZRA’s service fees are separate from visa, permit, medical, document, travel and other third-party costs.
