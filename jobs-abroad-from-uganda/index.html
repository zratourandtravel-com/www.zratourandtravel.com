Looking for jobs abroad from Uganda?

ZRA Tour & Travel is based in Uganda and supports applicants who want to explore lawful employment opportunities in other countries. We focus on realistic vacancy research and application support.

How the process works

1. Tell us your profile

Country of residence, education, skills, experience and preferred job.

2. Research suitable vacancies

We look for opportunities that match your profile and explain the available route.

3. Prepare your application

CV, cover letter and supporting information can be prepared for the opportunity.

4. Follow the application

Understand next steps, employer communication and relevant immigration processes.

Countries we may research

Depending on current vacancies and eligibility, our search can include Canada, the United Kingdom, Germany, Portugal, Poland, Italy, Croatia, Romania, Greece, Bulgaria, Czechia, Slovakia, Slovenia, Lithuania, Latvia, Estonia, Malta, Cyprus, Ireland, Australia and New Zealand, among others.

Important: A country being listed does not mean a suitable vacancy is currently available or that a visa/work permit will be approved.
