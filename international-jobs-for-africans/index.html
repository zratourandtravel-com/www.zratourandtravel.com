International jobs for African applicants

African applicants can face different eligibility, recruitment and immigration requirements when applying for work abroad. ZRA provides practical job-search and application support rather than promising employment.

What we can help with

Job opportunity research

Search for suitable vacancies based on your profile and preferred countries.

CV & cover letter

Prepare professional application documents for the role.

Application support

Assist with appropriate application routes and follow-up.

Visa/work-permit guidance

Explain process and document requirements without promising approval.

Job categories

Agriculture and farm work, warehouse and packing, hospitality, cleaning, manufacturing, food processing, driving and logistics, construction, skilled trades, healthcare, chefs and cooks, motor mechanics, IT/software and other professional roles, depending on available vacancies.

Important for applicants

Check the employer identity, job requirements and official application route. Be cautious of anyone offering a guaranteed job or visa in exchange for payment.

No guaranteed outcome: ZRA's service is job-search and application support. Hiring decisions are made by employers, while visa and work-permit decisions are made by the relevant authorities.
