Questions

Frequently asked questions.

Does ZRA only support African applicants?

No. We welcome applicants from different countries. Eligibility is determined by the requirements of each opportunity.

Can ZRA assist with professional careers?

Yes. Where suitable vacancies are available, we can research opportunities ranging from entry-level positions to skilled trades and professional careers.

Can ZRA help with professional or career jobs?

Yes. We are ready to support professional and career applicants as well as skilled, practical and entry-level job seekers, depending on the available vacancies and the applicant's qualifications and experience.

Does ZRA guarantee me a job?

No. We provide job-search and application support. The employer decides who is hired.

Does ZRA guarantee a visa or work permit?

No. Immigration decisions are made by the relevant authorities. We can help you understand the process and requirements.

How do I know an opportunity is worth considering?

Look for an identifiable employer or recognised recruitment route, clear requirements, a traceable application channel and a lawful employment pathway.

What information should I send ZRA?

Your full name, country of residence, preferred destination, education, skills, work experience and the type of job you are seeking.

How can I contact ZRA?
